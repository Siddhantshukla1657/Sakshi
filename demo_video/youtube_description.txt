Sakshi (साक्षी, Hindi for "witness") is an AI support agent for payments that is designed to be audited, not just smart. The AI proposes an action, a deterministic policy engine written in code verifies it, and only then does money move. Every step is sealed in a SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger. Team XLR8: Siddhant Shukla and Raunak Kumar Gupta, Paytm hackathon.

The demo walks one customer through a small refund the agent resolves on its own, a failed UPI payment handled in Hinglish, a mid-value return that a supervisor approves, and a prompt-injection attack that the policy engine blocks. It then shows Attack Mode, a tamper test on the audit ledger, the autonomy dial with its counterfactual simulator, and the 29-case safety eval suite (29 of 29 pass, zero unsafe actions).
